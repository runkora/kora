# Orbit Strategy & Discovery

Product-discovery outputs: market/competitor research, candidate roadmap pillars, and their
long-term product fit. This sits **in front of** the PRD → ADR workflow in
[`AGENTS.md`](../../AGENTS.md): discovery here produces pillars and PRDs; PRDs land in
[`docs/prd/`](../prd/) and architecture decisions follow in [`docs/adr/`](../adr/).

Most artifacts here are generated by the **`product-discovery`** skill
(`.claude/skills/product-discovery/`). See its `SKILL.md` for the pipeline (research → ideate →
synthesize → stress → pillar-map → PRD) and the thinking-lens library.

## Layout

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `opportunity-radar.md` | **Append-only** discovery radar — every scan's ranked opportunity slate; deferred directions are future seeds |
| `research/` | Research dossiers — competitive teardowns, landscape maps, screenshots |
| `pillars/` | Roadmap pillar / product-fit docs (the strategy artifacts) |

> Working exhaust (frames, raw per-lens ideation, fetched pages, screenshots-in-progress) lives
> in `.scratch/discovery/` and is intentionally **not** committed — only the durable artifacts
> above and PRDs in [`docs/prd/`](../prd/) persist.

Product pillars (from the Orbit hub): **Agents · Identity & Policy · Inference & Model Gateway ·
Audit & Governance · Playground · Context Lake · Infrastructure & Deployment · Enablement**
(+ Cross-pillar). Horizons: **Now / Next / Later**. Discovery candidates extend one of these or
open a coherent new one.

---

> **Framing reminder.** Every pillar serves Orbit's **dual user** — human operators *and* the
> autonomous agents that consume the platform (and, where relevant, the **enablement/blueprint**
> value for clients). The three questions (*Who is this agent? What can it access? How does it
> connect?*) are the founding spine and a coherence test — **not a ceiling**: pillars may open
> net-new ground beyond them. The one hard line is that nothing erodes the security guarantees
> (verifiable identity, pre-execution policy, no-creds-to-agents, traceability).
