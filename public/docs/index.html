❓ The Three Core Questions:

Everything in kora centers around answering these three questions for every agent action:

  1. Who is this agent? — SPIFFE identity certificate (unforgeable)
  2. What can it access? — OPA policy decision (checked before execution)
  3. How does it connect? — Injected credentials (never exposed)
